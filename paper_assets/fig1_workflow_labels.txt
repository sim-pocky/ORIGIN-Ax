ORIGIN-Ax workflow labels

1. Input mixed spatial transcriptomics expression and spatial coordinates.
2. Generate candidate axes from standard decompositions: ICA, NMF, high-target-evidence subset ICA, and high-target-evidence subset NMF.
3. Group candidate axes into axis families using loading similarity and top-gene overlap.
4. Score each family using target-origin evidence, off-target evidence, and consensus stability.
5. Apply target-specific off-target references for real-positive interpretation.
6. Report target-axis, off-target, downgraded, or non-identifiable families.
7. Restrict biological claims to allowed evidence levels.
